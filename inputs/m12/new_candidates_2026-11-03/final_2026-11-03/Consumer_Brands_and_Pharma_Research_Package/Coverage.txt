Consumer Brands and Pharma — Round 1 research notes
Research date: 3 November 2026

Scope
Consumer brands (6) researched first, then Pharmaceuticals and Biotechnology (6), using the supplied two-tab template. No scoring, ranking, recommendations, forecasts or target prices added.

Cutoff and periods
Annual reports published by 31 Oct 2026. FY0 is FY2025 except Constellation Brands FY2026; Lululemon FY2025 ends 1 Feb 2026. FY3 and FY1 dates appear on every evidence record. Later interim numbers are not substituted for annual inputs.

Units and basis
Financial amounts and shares are millions; EPS and prices are per ordinary share in reporting currency. GBP EPS quoted in pence divided by 100. Lululemon financial statements in thousands divided by 1,000. Cash capex is the positive magnitude of cash PPE purchases. No estimates or placeholder zeros. Lululemon zero debt is explicitly supported by undrawn facilities and operating-only leases.

Protected fields
WInS stays blank. market_cap_cutoff_proxy_m stays blank in CSV; the original Excel formulas remain. Payments-only software capex and non-ADS ratios remain blank. Every blank CSV field is itemized on MISSING, including intentional blanks.

Primary financial sources
All populated annual financial fields come from company annual reports or SEC filings. StockAnalysis, Yahoo financial statement summaries and StockTrak were not used for annual values. Vertex FY2022 was initially read from an AnnualReports mirror and then verified against the linked SEC filing; no annual amount remains secondary-only.

Secondary-only market data
All 30 Oct 2026 unadjusted equity closes and GBP/USD and USD/DKK daily FX closes are from Yahoo Finance public chart/history data only. The precise historical links are in Evidence and notes. Gold or StockTrak sign-in was not required. Share prices are restored to quoted cent precision from the API floats; FX and converted prices are retained to 8 decimals.

Acquisition screen
Y means no announced, outstanding takeover of the candidate was identified in the annual filings and company-announcement searches through 31 Oct 2026. This is a disclosure-review classification, not an explicit issuer assurance. Buyer-side acquisitions, minority investments and completed historical deals are not counted as the candidate being acquired. Supplemental source dates are in Evidence.

Roche security and proxy
The template ROG.SW identifier is retained to match the requested company. Roche announced replacement of Genussscheine by economically equivalent ROP participation certificates, 1:1, first trading 17 Mar 2026. ROP.SW supplies the cutoff price. All outstanding bearer and non-voting instruments are summed after consolidated treasury holdings. The preserved proxy formula uses the ROP price for the total and is not a class-specific market-cap sum.

Outstanding verification gaps
L'Oréal: PPE-only cash capex cannot be separated from the verified combined PPE-plus-intangibles cash line. Gilead: finance leases are unquantified, so strict total debt is blank. Roche: finance-lease receivables are disclosed, but the bank-like loan-book test remains unresolved. Separately labelled continuing EPS is blank wherever unverified.

Definitions
The two earlier package exceptions (combined capex and EVERTEC debt) were not extended to these candidates. This package requires strict PPE capex and US GAAP debt including finance leases. The original request and embedded prompt control over contradictory guide-sheet hints: unknowns stay blank, and total equity is the reported consolidated total, including non-controlling interests. The guide sheet itself is preserved. Operating income and CFO are reported without normalization.
