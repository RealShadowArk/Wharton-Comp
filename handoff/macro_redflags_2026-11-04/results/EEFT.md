## Ranked risks

1. **JUDGMENT · RED · Remittance profit takes longer to recover.** The guidance cut and 37% segment profit decline show how lower transfer counts can overwhelm digital growth. Tax, enforcement and Wise’s lower fees can add pressure. **Growth down. Margin down.** See #1, #14, #15 and #24.
2. **JUDGMENT · AMBER · European ATMs earn less per machine.** Fewer tourist withdrawals and tighter fee rules can reduce revenue while site and servicing costs persist. Over 2033–2042, payment habits matter more than a single travel season. **Growth down. Margin down.** See #12, #13 and #18.
3. **JUDGMENT · AMBER · CoreCard replaces Apple-related revenue too slowly.** The issuer transition creates a gap if new contracts ramp later or earn less than the business they replace. **Growth down. Margin down.** See #4.
4. **JUDGMENT · AMBER · ATM funding becomes more expensive.** The November deadlines arrive just after the freeze. If balances remain and lenders decline to renew, replacement borrowing can raise financing costs or consume cash. See #5, #6 and #17.
5. **JUDGMENT · AMBER · The model overstates cash available to shareholders.** ATM cash supports operations, disposal proceeds can be irregular, and note settlement can use cash or issue shares. The card’s cash-flow check leaves these questions open. See #5–#8.
