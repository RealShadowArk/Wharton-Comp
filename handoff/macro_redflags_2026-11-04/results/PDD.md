**FACT — Scope.** Wharton Global Youth investment team. Research cutoff: early 4 November 2026 UTC. Price baseline: 30 October U.S. close. Portfolio freeze: 6 November 2026. Funding horizon: 2033–2042. The team supplied the PDD card and macro notes in `macro_redflags_2026-11-04.zip`.

**JUDGMENT — RED.** The EU handling fee is the biggest change since the card’s price date. Temu’s share of spending in a five-country panel had already fallen after the July duty. The long-term question is whether Temu can preserve its price advantage after customs, local fulfillment and compliance costs. U.S. audience recovery shows the EU result cannot be applied to every market. See rows 9–12.
