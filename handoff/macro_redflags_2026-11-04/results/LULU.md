## Ranked flags and model failure modes

1. **JUDGMENT · RED · Demand recovery takes longer.** U.S. weakness, falling mainland China comparable sales and rival gains put both the near-term recovery and 2033–2042 growth at risk. Rows 4–5 and 26–30.
2. **JUDGMENT · AMBER · Recurring cash flow is weaker than the model assumes.** The two card ratios depend on the cash-flow baseline. Working-capital timing and the tariff refund make recent cash generation a weak basis for extrapolation. Rows 6 and 16.
3. **JUDGMENT · AMBER · Margins recover less than the model assumes.** The card’s implied-growth comparison holds margins at the latest full-year level. Lower refund-adjusted Q2 margin, duty exposure and competitive promotions put that assumption at risk. Rows 15–16 and 27–30.
4. **JUDGMENT · RED · The new lawsuit challenges the turnaround narrative.** Its allegations concern the same traffic and demand trends the model needs to reverse. The case adds near-term disclosure and litigation risk. Row 1.
5. **JUDGMENT · AMBER · Freeze-day handovers raise execution risk.** Product and supply-chain responsibilities are changing while the company needs to improve its assortment and manage landed costs. Rows 4, 9 and 15.
