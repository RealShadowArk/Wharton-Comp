# NVIDIA pre-freeze red-flag review

**FACT.** Prepared for the Wharton Global Youth investment team on November 4, 2026, before the U.S. trading session. The price comparison ends at the November 3 close. The portfolio freezes November 6, 2026. The funding window is 2033–2042. The team references are `companies/NVDA.md` and `macro_context.md` in `macro_redflags_2026-11-04.zip`.

**JUDGMENT.** The most important model risks are slower cash collection and commitments to fund customers, a shift in cloud spending toward competing chips, and memory costs that may be hard to pass through. The $105 billion Ohio guarantee creates a separate contingent cash exposure that can extend into the funding window.
