## 2. Ranked risks

The first three are the most likely ways the model could be wrong.

1. **JUDGMENT · RED · HOKA growth stays slower.** If weaker direct demand and rival cushioning launches persist after the holiday season, the consensus growth path may be too high. [Deckers Q2 release, Oct. 22, primary](https://ir.deckers.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0000910521-26-000028/deckex991pressrelease-9302.htm) · [Nike call, Oct. 1, primary](https://investors.nike.com/financial-information/financial-results/transcript/9591/q1-2027-transcript)
2. **JUDGMENT · AMBER · Recurring margins fall short.** Refunds lifted reported profitability while continuing duties and promotions limit what Deckers can retain from each sale.
