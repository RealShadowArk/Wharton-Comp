## Ranked flags

1. **JUDGMENT · AMBER · Cash conversion disappoints again.** The FY26 cash miss and lower FY27 guide make collections the most immediate test of the card at Nov. 5 earnings. [Rows 2–4 evidence][call26]
2. **JUDGMENT · AMBER · Cloud and AI fail to replace legacy profit.** High-margin support is shrinking and rivals are embedding AI into existing workflows. The end of enterprise cloud bookings disclosure makes the transition harder to track. [OpenText FY26 10-K][k] · [Investor slides][slides] · [Microsoft rollout][msft] · [IBM launch][ibm]
3. **JUDGMENT · AMBER · Refinancing gets harder if cash weakens.** October reduced near-term maturity pressure. Floating-rate exposure remains, and the October 2033 notes mature at the start of the funding window. [OpenText 8-K][refi] · [FY26 10-K][k] · [Fitch][fitch]
4. **JUDGMENT · AMBER · Canada tax dispute absorbs cash.** The planned payment falls inside 12 months and an adverse outcome could increase cash taxes over several years. [FY26 10-K, Note 14][k]
5. **JUDGMENT · AMBER · Asset sales reduce the earnings available to service debt.** Divestiture proceeds may lower debt while the earnings base also contracts. [OpenText FY26 10-K][k] · [Fitch credit analysis][fitch]
