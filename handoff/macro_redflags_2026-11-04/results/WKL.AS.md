## Ranked flags

1. **JUDGMENT · AMBER · Clinical AI renewals.** UAMS shows a path from free alternatives to a lost renewal. Duke and the NHS renewal show WKL can still retain customers. Rows 23, 26 and 27.
2. **JUDGMENT · AMBER · Margin and cash baseline.** The disposal gain and ongoing capitalized software investment could make the bear case too generous if the model carries through the gain or underfunds development. Rows 7–9.
3. **JUDGMENT · AMBER · Legal and tax competition.** RELX and TRI are placing research and drafting in the same workflow, which could weaken renewals and sales of additional WKL modules. Rows 23–25 and 27.
4. **JUDGMENT · AMBER · Hospital budgets.** Medicaid changes could make U.S. hospital customers more price-sensitive during the long horizon, even with the Medicare inpatient offset. Rows 20–21.
5. **JUDGMENT · AMBER · Pre-freeze disclosure.** The nine-month update can test whether the August group guide held and whether longer sales cycles spread beyond Corporate Performance & ESG. Rows 2, 4 and 22.
