## Ranked risks

1. **JUDGMENT · RED — Recurring cash conversion stays weak.** The cash check is most vulnerable if lower post-sale operating cash persists and stranded costs prove harder to remove. Rows 5–7.
2. **JUDGMENT · RED — Core growth falls short of the assumed recovery.** A slow Lysol recovery, recurring promotions and weaker China supplement growth could lower the sustainable revenue path. Rows 3–4 and 30–34.
3. **JUDGMENT · RED — Formula litigation consumes material cash.** The federal defence verdict reduces immediate pressure, while state appeals, a growing federal docket and the separate securities case leave cash exposure open. Rows 14–15.
4. **JUDGMENT · RED — Hormuz disruption compresses margins.** Reckitt has already raised selected European prices, and further pass-through could cost volume. Rows 23–25.
5. **JUDGMENT · AMBER — Higher refinancing costs absorb more cash.** The 2027 dollar maturity and higher 2027 finance-expense guidance increase the cash generation required to support the case. Rows 9–10 and 20–22.
