2. **JUDGMENT · RED — Lower growth ceiling.** The October guide and bookings make the card vulnerable if its consensus path still assumes July’s stronger outlook. Rows 3 and 5.

5. **JUDGMENT · AMBER — Legal and tax cash exposure.** The November stay buys time. It leaves the penalty unresolved, while the separate tax deposit and U.S. cases carry their own cash risks. Rows 11–13 and 28.
