## Team rules (all packages)

- **Approved universe:** stocks, ETFs and Treasuries available on WInS, priced at $5 or more. Nothing else.
- **Labels:** label every figure **CASE / RESEARCH / ASSUMPTION** and give its source (filing, page, date). Write **MISSING** rather than guessing. Never fill a gap with a zero or an average.
- **AI use:** AI may find data and explain ideas, but **every figure must be checked against the filing**, and the notes must be in your own words. Keep a short log of which AI tool you used and for what: the Final Report must cite AI use in APA.
- **Do not decide buy or sell.** You collect evidence; Pat runs the models and decides.

## What to collect for each company

1. **Valuation inputs (Round 2 template):**
   - latest fiscal-year revenue, EBIT, D&A, capex, change in working capital and tax rate;
   - analyst consensus revenue growth for the next 3 years (source and date);
   - cash, debt (including finance leases), diluted shares;
   - the price on the date you collect.
2. **Bank-like companies (banks, insurers) instead need:**
   - book value per share;
   - net income and average equity for the last 3 years (for ROE);
   - dividends per share.
   - Mark them **RI method**.
3. **REITs instead need:**
   - FFO and AFFO for the last 3 years (company supplement);
   - dividends per share, net debt, diluted shares.
   - Mark them **FFO method**.
4. **Red-flag lines:** the inputs for the Piotroski and Beneish checks (template "Red flags" tab), for the last 2 years.
5. **Risk:** the worst calendar-year total return in USD since listing, with the year (for the 2% position cap).
6. **Management evidence:** one line of evidence for each of four items, **without scoring them** (Pat grades):
   - capital allocation;
   - guidance record (beats and misses over the last 8 quarters);
   - governance (board, share classes, related parties);
   - controversies and legal.
7. **AI exposure:** is this company an AI beneficiary, an AI adopter, or non-AI? Name one way AI could replace its products. One line each.

Names marked **(have)** already have DCF inputs in `inputs/m03/`. Only update the price, guidance and anything new since 3 Nov.
