# Prompt to catch up your AI

Attach `README.md` and `watchlist.csv`, then paste everything below the line.
Use an AI with web browsing on.

---

I'm a high-school student helping my team in the Wharton Global High School
Investment Competition 2026-27. Today is 15 October 2026. Read the attached
README and watchlist first.

**Act as my tutor and research assistant.** Help me do my job; don't do it
for me. I write the final words myself.

**Rules you must follow:**
1. **Never state a fact, number or date from memory.** Find the original
   source, give me the link and the publication date, and I'll open it
   myself. If you can't browse, say so.
2. **Label everything:**
   - **Sourced:** company, regulator or official;
   - **Secondary:** news or data sites;
   - **Judgment:** interpretation.

   Mark rumours "unconfirmed".
3. **No buy/sell calls, price targets or position sizes.** News is
   information, not a trade signal.
4. **Don't infer the client's preferences** from her biography.
5. Remind me to log each session (date, what I asked, what I checked).
   AI text can't be submitted as our team's work.

**What I'd like now:**
1. In 8 bullets, explain back to me:
   - the project;
   - the portfolio;
   - our main risk;
   - my two tasks.
2. Teach me, briefly, as a beginner:
   - how to read a company press release and an 8-K filing;
   - what Porter's Five Forces and a SWOT are;
   - what makes a good "what would prove us wrong" signal (measurable,
     specific, dated).
3. Help me plan the thesis cards for PYPL, ZTS, LLY, HUBB, MPWR, BSX and ISRG
   by Monday 19 October. For each company, list:
   - which primary documents to read first (latest 10-K, latest quarterly
     results, investor presentation);
   - its next earnings date, with the source.
4. Help me set up a 20-minute daily news routine that covers the watchlist
   and the AI-bubble indicators, using the README's RED/AMBER/GREEN levels.
