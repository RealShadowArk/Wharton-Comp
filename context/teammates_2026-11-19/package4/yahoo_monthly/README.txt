WHARTON PACKAGE 4 | MONTHLY PRICE HISTORY
Research snapshot: November 19, 2026

These CSVs were generated from Yahoo Finance's chart data at monthly frequency
for the provider's maximum available monthly range. Each price series uses
the U.S. listing in USD. Yahoo labels each monthly bar with its first date;
Close and Adj Close reflect the final session of that bar. November 2026 is
partial. Use December adjusted closes through 2025 for complete-year returns.
The workbook documents early daily/dividend supplements for older histories.
SPY is the market benchmark. All listings use USD, so no FX series is needed.

The original Yahoo Historical Data Max Monthly CSVs were downloaded into
Chrome on the connected computer. This archive contains chart-derived CSVs.
Source URL, date coverage and row count are in Sources.csv.
