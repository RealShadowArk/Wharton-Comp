METHOD AND LIMITS

The more specific READMEs govern: ranges are retained as numeric low/high endpoints in AG:AH, with exact wording and basis in X. W is blank because a single point was not supplied. No growth assumption was invented.

The consistent consensus source displays years 1 and 2, but year 3 requires an upgrade. CEG/NRG year-1 consensus growth is calculated by the provider against REPORTED FY2025 revenue. It must not be compounded on the workbook's pro forma base. Notes preserve the displayed consensus revenue amounts so the team can reconcile the bases without silently mixing them.

Edwards continuing-only CFO is also not separately verified. Net interest income/expense is not relabeled as gross amounts.
