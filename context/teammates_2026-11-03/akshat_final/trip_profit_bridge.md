## Overlap and tax checks

- **MakeMyTrip is inside the RMB 15,409m line.** Trip.com sold 34,372,221 Class B shares for about USD 3.0bn when MakeMyTrip repurchased them in July 2025. The filing identifies roughly RMB 15.2bn of gain inside the broader line and reports the individual transaction only at that rounded precision. Sale proceeds, gain and remaining equity stake measure three different things. [Form 20-F significant transactions and Note 7](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1269238/000119312526183379/d27369d20f.htm#:~:text=34%2C372%2C221).
- **Vendor gain subtotal is RMB 19,471m.** This equals 15,409 + 3,954 + 143 − 35. Adding 400 of investment dividends yields 19,871, consistent with the issuer's rounded RMB 19.9bn earnings-release amount. The 19,471 and 19.9bn figures are alternative aggregations of overlapping items. Note 17 separately reports an exchangeable senior note fair-value loss of 861, already included in the net 3,954. Arithmetically this implies a 4,815 residual equity-security contribution on the combined line, rather than an independently reported standalone exact number. [StockAnalysis annual view](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tcom/financials/income-statement/) · [Issuer FY2025 release](https://ctripcominternationalltd.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/tripcom-group-limited-reports-unaudited-fourth-quarter-and-5) · [Audited Note 17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1269238/000119312526183379/d27369d20f.htm#:~:text=the%20change%20in%20fair%20value%20loss).
- **Vendor interest-and-investment income is RMB 3,003m.** This combines audited interest income of 2,603 with investment dividends of 400. The 353 in affiliate earnings is separately presented. The audited operating revenue line also includes interest on financial services receivables. Keep that operating interest distinct from the 2,603 nonoperating interest line. [Audited filing and accounting policies](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1269238/000119312526183379/d27369d20f.htm) · [StockAnalysis annual view](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tcom/financials/income-statement/).
- **Disposal tax is separately identifiable.** The audited cash-flow reconciliation and tax note identify RMB 2,073m capital-gain tax on the equity-method investment disposal. It is within audited FY2025 income tax expense of 5,815. Normalizing pretax and post-tax earnings uses different bridge rows. For an EBIT-based DCF, the audited 15,773 operating profit already excludes the other-income items above. [Audited cash-flow statement and Note 15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1269238/000119312526183379/d27369d20f.htm#:~:text=Tax%20on%20capital%20gain%20of%20disposal).
