# Round 2 pharma evidence

Research cutoff October 31, 2026. Dates are issuer expectations as of the cited release. A patent date, a clinical result, a submission and a regulator's decision are separate milestones.

## Novartis (NVS)

- **Patent anchors.** The 2025 Form 20-F lists U.S. Cosentyx 2029, Kesimpta 2031, Kisqali 2031 and Pluvicto 2034 with a term extension application pending. The U.S. Entresto combination patent is already expired. EU dates for the same products differ. [2025 Form 20-F, pp. 37–38](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1114448/000111444826000004/nvs-20251231.htm).
- **Upcoming data.** The October 27 results deck lists 2026 ianalumab in first-line immune thrombocytopenia, Leqvio ORION-4 cardiovascular outcomes and Rhapsido in hidradenitis suppurativa. Its 2027 calendar includes event-driven abelacimab in stroke prevention in atrial fibrillation, Fabhalta in generalized myasthenia gravis, ianalumab in three autoimmune indications and Leqvio VICTORION-2 PREVENT. [Q3 investor presentation, slide 17](https://www.novartis.com/sites/novartis_com/files/q3-2026-investor-presentation.pdf#page=17).
- **FY2026 guidance at constant currency.** Net sales growth low single digits and core operating income flat to up low single digits. Q3 results explicitly separate recent positive remibrutinib Phase III data from the unsuccessful primary endpoint on del-desiran in myotonic dystrophy type 1. [October 27 results](https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-delivered-sales-growth-and-core-margin-expansion-q3-raised-fy-core-operating-income-guidance).
- **September 30 balance-sheet bridge.** Cash USD 8,870m. Noncurrent financial debt USD 36,699m, current financial debt and derivatives USD 9,650m, separately reported leases USD 1,961m and company-defined net debt USD 37,374m. Ordinary shares outstanding excluding treasury stock were 1,888,623,572. Use the relevant gross-debt and cash fields in the DCF to avoid counting cash twice. [Condensed financial report, pp. 21 and 52](https://www.novartis.com/sites/novartis_com/files/q3-2026-interim-financial-report-en.pdf).

## AstraZeneca (AZN)

- **Patent anchors.** The issuer's 2025 patent supplement lists U.S. Tagrisso patents in 2032 and 2035. Use the supplement's product-by-country columns and notes for the particular claim type. AstraZeneca's October 30 results already identify Farxiga and Brilinta loss of exclusivity as year-to-date headwinds. [2025 patent supplement, p. 3](https://www.astrazeneca.com/content/dam/az/Investor_Relations/annual-report-2025/pdf/AstraZeneca_Patent_Expiries_of_Key_Marketed_Products_2025.pdf#page=3) · [October 30 results](https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/press-releases/2026/9m-and-q3-2026-results.html).
- **Trial calendar.** The October 30 appendix schedules data from the Phase III Bluestar-Endometrial01 study of puxitatug samrotecan in H2 2027. The latest disclosed Tagrisso plus Orpathys SAFFRON result was already reported October 25 and showed progression-free and overall survival benefit in the tested MET-driven lung cancer population. The SERENA-4 Phase III trial did not meet its primary progression-free survival endpoint. [Q3 clinical appendix, p. 32](https://www.astrazeneca.com/content/dam/az/PDF/2026/9mq3/9M-and-Q3-2026-results-clinical-trials-appendix.pdf#page=32) · [SAFFRON October 25](https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/press-releases/2026/tagrisso-plus-orpathys-reduced-progression-or-death-risk-by-45-and-death-risk-by-32-in-met-driven-egfrm-nsclc-after-progression-on-tagrisso.html) · [Q3 Form 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/901832/000165495426009256/a0835b.htm).
- **FY2026 guidance at constant exchange rates.** Total revenue growth mid-to-high single digits, core EPS growth low double digits, core tax rate 18–22%. [October 30 results](https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/press-releases/2026/9m-and-q3-2026-results.html).
- **September 30 balance-sheet bridge.** Cash USD 7,936m and company-defined gross debt USD 34,191m including USD 2,777m of IFRS 16 leases. The borrowing-only components total USD 31,414m. Company-defined net debt was USD 25,833m. The September 30 voting-rights disclosure reports 1,551,053,289 ordinary shares and zero in treasury. [Q3 financial announcement, table 13 and note 3](https://www.astrazeneca.com/content/dam/az/PDF/2026/9mq3/9M-and-Q3-2026-results-announcement.pdf) · [September share capital filing mirror](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/AZN/6-k-astrazeneca-plc-current-report-foreign-issuer-738355687c40.html).

## Sanofi (SNY)

- **Patent anchors.** Dupixent compound protection is listed in the 2025 Form 20-F through March 2031 in the U.S. with patent-term extension, September 2032 in the EU with supplemental protection certificates, and May 2034 in Japan with extension. The EU pediatric extension to March 2033 was still being granted across countries at filing. [Sanofi 2025 20-F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1121404/000162828026008403/sny-20251231.htm) · [Regeneron 2025 10-K, U.S. date March 28, 2031](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/872589/000087258926000008/regn-20251231.htm).
- **Upcoming data.** The October 30 Q3 calendar lists Phase 3 Sarclisa in transplant-eligible multiple myeloma for 2026. Its 2027 list includes Anxu in third-line graft-versus-host disease, fitusiran in hemophilia A/B and riliprubart in chronic inflammatory demyelinating polyneuropathy. Frexalimab Phase 3 multiple sclerosis data are listed for 2028. [October 30 results, R&D milestones](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1121404/000112140426000009/pressreleaseq32026english.htm).
- **FY2026 guidance at constant exchange rates.** Sales growth low teens and business EPS growth mid teens. The release estimates October exchange rates would subtract about one percentage point from each at actual rates. [October 30 results](https://www.sanofi.com/en/media-room/press-releases/2026/2026-10-30-06-30-00-3392028).
- **September 30 balance-sheet bridge.** Cash EUR 5,966m. Gross short- and long-term borrowings EUR 17,942m (3,349m current plus 14,593m long-term), excluding separately reported leases. Company-defined net debt EUR 12,201m. Investor relations reports 1,207,773,393 issued shares; confirm the treasury-share count before treating issued shares as outstanding. The Q3 weighted average of 1,200.0m is a separate EPS figure. [Q3 results, appendices 5–6](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1121404/000112140426000009/pressreleaseq32026english.htm) · [September share capital](https://www.sanofi.com/en/investors/sanofi-share-and-adrs/shares-structure-vote).
