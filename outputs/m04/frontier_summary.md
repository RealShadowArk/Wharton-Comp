## How to read this

- The classic frontier piles into whatever did best in the sample; the p10-p90 column shows how far those weights swing when the same months are resampled. That instability is the standard reason not to set weights with an optimizer, and the reason we size by the floor rule and the 2% worst-year loss instead.
- IEF stands in for the Treasury ladder. The ladder is held to maturity to match the payments, so its market-price swings do not change what Laura receives; the whole-portfolio view therefore overstates the cost of holding bonds.
- Files: `frontier_points_<start>_<all|growth>.csv` (curves and our mix, for the chart).
