Student judgments (Pat 20 Nov): every run is shown twice, with and without the Great Depression years; block bootstrap of S&P 500 years with the mean set to M02 LOW/BASE/HIGH; yearly rebalancing; hold-back 0% (10% and 20% shown). **Simulation results do not prove future outcomes.**

## What is simulated

- **Laura's plan, not the WInS account:** contributions of $300,000 (2027) and $150,000 (2028), $450,000 in total (CASE); ten $50,000 payments 2033-2042 (CASE). WInS gains and losses are never added (competition rule).
- **The stock sleeve is the S&P 500**, the longest reliable history (1928-2025), with its average set to M02. Our specific holdings are not modelled; a variant with our final mix waits for the 2028 additions.
- Floor rule: stocks = 2 x (wealth - floor), at most 100% of wealth; the rest earns 5.24% (locked ladder yield); rebalanced yearly.
- Required reserve at the start of 2033: $401,623. Floor at the start of 2028: $311,111.
- **Breach point:** the floor breaks only if stocks fall more than 47.4% in one year.
