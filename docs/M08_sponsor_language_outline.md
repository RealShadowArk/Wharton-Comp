# M08 sponsor language: checklist

What the co-sponsor wording has to do. The wording itself goes in
`M08_sponsor_language_draft.md`, written in my own words once the numbers
pass validation (M09).

## Must include

- A dollar range, not one exact number (case p.3).
- How confident the team is that the 2033 gift lands in that range, stated
  as a modeled estimate.
- What would push the gift toward the low or high end.
- That the ten operating payments are funded first.

## Must avoid

- Wording that sounds like a promise or a guarantee.
- Any number not traceable to a released model version.
- A confidence figure for the gift presented as if it were the confidence
  that the payments will be made. They are separate numbers.
- Facility cost estimates or outside funding totals (out of scope, case
  p.4).

## Check before sending to P5

- Every number matches `outputs/M08_2031_ranges.csv` and names its
  version.
- The range method and coverage match `M08_policy_specification.json`.
- If the lower bound is $0, the wording says so plainly.
