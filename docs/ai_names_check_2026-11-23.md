### What drives each result

- **NVDA:**
  - The same pass and Beneish flag as on 3 Nov. The flag is mechanical: the sales-growth index is very high.
  - The price is 166.37, down from 188.10 paid on 6 Nov and 204.64 on 30 Oct.
  - Consensus is Akshaj K's of 3 Nov.
- **AMD:**
  - GAAP operating margin is 10.7%, after about $2.4bn of acquired-intangible amortization.
  - Year 1 = Q1-Q3 2026 actual plus Q4 consensus ($51.6bn, +48.8%).
  - Years 2-3 consensus is MISSING, so a new TEST rule applies: the fade starts after year 1. That rule makes the path more generous, not less, and AMD still fails.
- **Intel:**
  - FY2025 operating income is -$23m, so the operating value is about zero before the debt and minority-interest claims ($12.1bn of fab stakes, Mobileye and IMS are counted as senior claims).
  - The tax rate is 21% (ASSUMPTION), because pretax income includes gains while operating income is negative.
- **CoreWeave:** it is in its build-out phase (capex $10.3bn on revenue $5.1bn). Holding that ratio for ten years gives a large negative value, so the standard method cannot value it. Finance leases are MISSING.
- **Micron:** FY2028-29 consensus is MISSING, and the standard fade from +106% would compound revenue about 60-fold. Two TESTs, neither is the standard method:
  - **TEST A:**
    - FY2027 consensus (+106%, $275bn), then 3% a year, at the FY2026 operating margin of 74.6%.
    - It passes, but only because the peak margin is held for ten years.
  - **TEST B:**
    - The same path at the simple average FY2023-26 operating margin of 17.8% (-34.8%, 5.2%, 26.1%, 74.6%).
    - Value is about zero, because boom-level capex (23% of revenue) is kept.
    - It is a harsh stress, not a fair value.
  - Micron's value rests almost entirely on how long memory margins stay near the peak. FY2023 was an operating loss.
- **Palantir:**
  - Strong growth (+84% in 2026, +50% in 2027), but the price is about 85 times FY2025 revenue.
  - Its 6-year beta of 2.38 is higher than its 5-year beta of 1.64.

## Data and limits

- **Statements:** Yahoo fundamentals-timeseries, annual (`tools/yahoo_round2_workbook.py` writes `inputs/round2_ai_2026-11-23/Yahoo_Round2_data.xlsx` and the raw `yahoo_statements.json`). RESEARCH, standardized by Yahoo. They are not yet checked against the 10-Ks, so every result here is TEST.
- **Debt:** current plus long-term debt, plus minority interests. Finance leases are MISSING because Yahoo does not separate them from operating leases. Dilution is MISSING (shares outstanding are used).
- **Consensus:** `inputs/round2_ai_2026-11-23/consensus.csv`, one source per row.
  - The sources are web articles of different dates, because StockAnalysis and Yahoo's analysis pages are blocked from this environment.
  - 2026 figures were checked against Q1-Q3 2026 actuals. AMD's older $44.1bn estimate was stale, so actuals plus the Q4 estimate are used.
- **Benchmarks:** betas in the valuation are against SPY (the Round 2 method); betas in the screen are against ACWI (the universe screen method). That is why they differ.
- **Not run:** M12 (no peer file) and management grades.
