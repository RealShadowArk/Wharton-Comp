# Finalists and close calls after the universe screen (19 Nov 2026, TEST)

## How to read this

**Two strategies:**
- **Ours (refined):** beta below the industry average.
- **The winner's:** also alpha above the industry average.

Both then apply the same value tests.

**Measures:**
- **Alpha and beta:** 60 monthly USD returns against ACWI (alpha annualized), compared with the GICS sub-industry average. Source: `outputs/screen/universe_screen_2026-11-17.csv`.
- **Value/price:** at beta 1.0, the 5-year beta and the full-history beta, on 30 Oct prices.
  - "Bear" is the uniform DRAFT bear case divided by the price.
  - **Pass** = value above price at all three betas, and upside/downside of at least 1.0.
- **Red flag rule:** Piotroski below 4, or Beneish above −1.78. Neither is applied to insurers or REITs.
- **Company red flags:** taken from the teammates' evidence lines.

Values are research estimates, never price targets, returns or position sizes. Fox is dropped (Pat, 19 Nov).

### Pass both strategies

**Arch Capital (ACGL)**
- **Strong:**

  - very low beta;
  - M12 84.7;
  - upside/downside 2.80.
- **Weak:** the bear case is below the price (0.95). Catastrophe-loss years are smoothed by the 3-year ROE average.
- **Stands out:** alpha +17.2%.
- **Red flag:** none from our tests (not applied to insurers). Governance: a staggered board.

**Cigna (CI)**
- **Strong:**
  - 4.00× at its 5-year beta (0.25);
  - bear 1.29, so no downside even in the bear case;
  - after the price fell to $273 by 19 Nov, 2.06×.
- **Weak:**
  - **The teammate's residual-income rows value it at $167–196 against a $298 price (fails).** Book value includes Express Scripts goodwill.
