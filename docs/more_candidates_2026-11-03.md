# More companies to research, by industry (3 Nov 2026)

These are research leads, not recommendations. Each one is new: none was in Round 1, the teammates' lists or the 36 earlier companies. Suggestions come from what each business does and how it differs from what we already cover. No 2026 prices or results were checked.

## Before spending time on any name, check four things

1. **WInS:** the exact listing is on WInS at $5 or more (or the local equivalent).
2. **Size and cash:** market cap of at least $2bn, and positive free cash flow in the latest annual report (the team screen).
3. **Still independent:** it is not being acquired or taken private. Several 2025 deals removed candidates, for example Kenvue, Hologic, Skyworks/Qorvo and Electronic Arts, so none of those is listed here.
4. **Overlap:** it is not already heavy in VOO's top 10 or VXUS's top holdings. Note where a name adds to the AI exposure we already hold.

Foreign listings follow our currency rule: local price, local financials and the local risk-free rate.

Rule 0.3 still applies: bank-like businesses (deposits or loan books inside operating cash flow) are left out. That is why Affirm, SoFi and Nu are not suggested.
